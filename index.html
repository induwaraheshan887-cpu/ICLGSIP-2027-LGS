About ICLGSIP 2027

Empowering Operational Excellence & Decarbonization

ICLGSIP 2027 brings together leading academic scientists, industrial researchers, operational pioneers, and policymakers to exchange breakthrough insights on Lean Operations, Green Engineering, and Digital Transformation.

In an era marked by resource volatility and climate urgency, this conference addresses how modern industrial enterprises can harmonize waste reduction, circular economy models, AI-driven manufacturing, and eco-friendly logistics to achieve long-term sustainability.

Call for Abstracts

Five Conference Themes

Authors and researchers are invited to submit abstracts aligned with the following five core themes:

1. Lean Excellence

Toyota Production System, 5S, Kaizen, Value Stream Mapping (VSM), Total Productive Maintenance (TPM), waste reduction, and operational flow optimization.

2. Quality & Productivity

Six Sigma, Total Quality Management (TQM), Statistical Process Control, Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE), defect reduction, and throughput metrics.

3. Lean Transformation

Organizational change management, lean leadership, workforce capability building, continuous improvement culture, and strategy deployment (Hoshin Kanri).

4. Circularity & Sustainability (Green)

Circular economy frameworks, green manufacturing, carbon accounting, renewable energy adoption, zero waste recovery, and eco-design systems.

5. Lean–Green Integration

Harmonizing lean waste elimination with green environmental metrics, energy-efficient automation, sustainable supply chains, and net-zero productivity.
